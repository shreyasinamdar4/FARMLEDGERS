About FarmLedgers

The FarmLedgers team is a group of farmers, developers, and agricultural experts who work together to improve the way agricultural supply chains function. The team believes that data and modern technology can make farming more transparent, efficient, and fair. With experience in blockchain and software development, the team has built a simple and farmer-friendly platform to replace complex spreadsheets and outdated systems. FarmLedgers uses technologies like blockchain, RFID, and real-time sensor data to securely track crops and monitor their quality at every stage. Special focus is given to creating easy-to-use interfaces and clear decision-support tools so that farmers and other stakeholders can understand and use the system without difficulty. The main goal of the team is to connect real farming activities with digital records, ensuring trust, transparency, and efficiency across the entire agricultural supply chain.
